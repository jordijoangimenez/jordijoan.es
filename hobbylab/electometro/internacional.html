    How it is calculated

    One poll per pollster

    Only the latest poll of each polling company counts, so those that publish every week do not weigh more than the rest.

    Weight

    A poll weighs more the more recent it is (half every 30 days) and the larger its sample (square root, between 300 and 3,000 interviews).

    Seats

    Weighted average of the midpoint of each pollster's range, allocated up to the size of the chamber by largest remainders. It is not a constituency-by-constituency simulation.

    Today, week, month

    "Today" is the weighted average of the whole term, with more weight for recent polls. "This week" and "This month" use only the polls published in the last 7 or 30 days, one per pollster, weighted by sample size.

    Term

    The average starts again after each election. Internal polls, exit polls and results are left out.

    Official result

    The result of the last election is the one Electogrāfica quotes in each poll as a reference; it has not been downloaded separately from the Interior Ministry. Check it against the official source before quoting it.

    Coalitions

    Adds up the average seats of the chosen parties. "Minimal majorities" are combinations that reach a majority with no party to spare. "In X of N polls" counts the pollsters whose latest seat estimate gives a majority (midpoint of their range). These are arithmetic sums, not likely pacts.

    Limits

    The data is extracted automatically from Electogrāfica's texts and may contain reading errors. Check each figure against the link to the original poll.
