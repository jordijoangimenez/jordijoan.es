ATM labels

The postage labels a machine prints with the value chosen on the spot: what they are, which machines Correos has used in Spain, how to read their code and where to look up each issue. These labels are not approved in the BOE, Spain's official gazette, so they are not part of the Sellómetro's catalogue of issues.

What they are

ATM comes from the German Automatenmarken, “machine stamps”. They are self-adhesive or gummed labels that a vending machine prints on the spot with the postage the customer asks for. The background design may come pre-printed on the roll or be printed in full, and the value and a code are added when printing. Correos calls them etiquetas franqueadoras; collectors also speak of variable-value stamps.

They are collected by design, by machine, by value and by the code on each label, which identifies the machine, the month and the purchase session.

How to read a current label

Each label from the Punto Filatélico carries a sequence like this one:

B9ES16ES010001001

B
Machine type (P for orders)
9
Month of purchase
ES
Country
16
Year
ES01
Machine number
0001
Session number, which goes up with each purchase
001
Label number within the session (up to 875 per session)

So every label has a code that never repeats.

Rates and formats

The machine uses the Correos rates of each year, identified by a letter. In 2026 they are: A €0.96, A2 €1.15, B €2.00 and C €2.10.

  • Strip of four labels: one at each rate (A, A2, B and C) or four at rate A.
  • Single labels: at any rate, as many as you like, with the design available.

The printing is thermal and stays legible for about ten years. Recent labels measure 55 × 25 mm, and Correos has a special postmark for them.

Where to get them

  • At philatelic fairs and exhibitions where Correos sets up the Punto Filatélico, with designs of their own for each event.
  • At the Correos office at Calle Conde de Peñalver 19, Madrid, where the machine is permanent (Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 14:00). Payment by card.
  • By order from Correos, for those who cannot go to a fair, with a minimum of €100 plus postage. The conditions are on the Correos ATM labels page (in Spanish).
