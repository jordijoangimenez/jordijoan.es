      How it works

      Source

      Every Spanish postage stamp issue is approved by a provision published in the Boletín Oficial del Estado (BOE), Spain's official gazette: a ministerial order until the 1990s, and a joint resolution of the competent under-secretariats since. The catalogue goes through the daily summaries of the BOE since 1960 with its open data API and collects every provision on issuing stamps, postcards and envelopes.

      Copied, not rewritten

      The series name, the text that presents each issue, its first day of sale and its technical details (printing, paper, perforation, size, face value, print run) are copied from the BOE, so they appear in Spanish. Where the BOE later published a correction, the issue links to it.

      Before 1995

      For almost every provision before 1995 the BOE only offers a scanned PDF. For those issues the catalogue shows the official title, the publication date and the link to the PDF, without technical details.

      Before 1960

      The BOE open data starts in 1960. Earlier issues, from Isabel II to the post-war years, and the few later ones that are not in the BOE come from the Catálogo de Sellos de España by FESOFI, with just what is needed to identify them: date, series, subject and face value of each stamp. The full record is on FESOFI.

      Pictures

      Each BOE issue is matched to its stamps in FESOFI by date and series name, and shows their pictures and the list of its values. When FESOFI has no picture (the newest issues, a few recent ones, Andorra, prepaid postcards), it shows the official picture from the Correos Filatelia website, matched by date and title; Correos publishes them from 1997 on. The matching is automatic: an issue may have no pictures or, when the catalogues name it differently, appear twice.

      Pictures and rights

      This is an informative, non-commercial catalogue. The pictures are shown straight from the FESOFI and Correos websites, not copied: this site keeps none of them, and when you open the page your browser asks their servers for them. If they stop loading, each issue is drawn as a text stamp. The design of each stamp belongs to its authors and to Correos, and the photographs to FESOFI and Correos. If you hold the rights to anything on this page and want it removed, open an issue on GitHub and it will be taken down.

      Topics

      Each issue is sorted into one or more topics (art, nature, people, sport…) from the words of its official text. This is an automatic, unofficial classification, so a few issues may sit in a surprising place.

      Territories

      Besides Spain, the BOE covers the issues of the Spanish post in Andorra and, until the 1970s, those of the Spanish territories in Africa (Sahara, Ifni, Guinea). They can be filtered separately.

      ATM labels

      Variable-value postage labels are not published in the BOE. They have their own page, with their history, how to read them and where to look up each issue: ATM labels.
